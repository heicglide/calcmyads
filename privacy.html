Privacy Policy

How CalcMyAds handles calculator inputs and website data.

Effective date: September 20, 2026

Calculator inputs

Calculator inputs are processed locally in your browser. CalcMyAds does not send the numbers you enter to Google Analytics or to a CalcMyAds server.

Optional analytics

With your permission, CalcMyAds uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how the website is used and improve its tools. Analytics may collect information such as page views, scrolling, outbound link clicks, referral source, browser and device details, and approximate location derived from network information. We do not intentionally send calculator values, names, email addresses, or other direct identifiers to Analytics.

Google processes Analytics data under its own terms and privacy policy. Advertising storage, Google signals, and ad-personalization signals are disabled in our Analytics configuration.

Your choice and browser storage

Analytics does not load unless you select “Accept analytics.” If you decline, the Google Analytics script is not loaded. Your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so the site can remember it.

You can review or change your choice at any time using the link in the footer. You can also clear this website’s stored data in your browser settings.

Cookies

If you accept analytics, Google Analytics may use first-party cookies or similar browser storage for measurement. CalcMyAds does not currently use advertising cookies or display third-party advertising.

External links

External services have their own privacy practices. Review their policies before providing personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or data practices change. The effective date above indicates the latest version.

Contact

Use the contact page for privacy questions.
