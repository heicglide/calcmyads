CPM formula explained

Learn how to calculate CPM, campaign cost, and impressions with practical examples.

The CPM formula

CPM = (campaign cost ÷ impressions) × 1,000

If a campaign costs $1,250 and delivers 250,000 impressions, its CPM is $5.00.

Reverse formulas

Cost = CPM × impressions ÷ 1,000

Impressions = budget ÷ CPM × 1,000
