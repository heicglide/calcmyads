Frequently asked questions

Questions about Impressions

How are impressions estimated?

Divide campaign budget by CPM, then multiply by 1,000.

Are impressions the same as people reached?

No. One person may generate multiple impressions.

Will the platform deliver the exact estimate?

Not necessarily. Auctions, targeting, pacing, and inventory affect delivery.
