Frequently asked questions

Questions about CPM

What is CPM?

CPM means cost per mille, or cost per one thousand ad impressions.

How do I calculate CPM?

Divide total campaign cost by total impressions, then multiply by 1,000.

Is a lower CPM always better?

Not necessarily. A lower CPM can reduce reach costs, but audience quality, placement, viewability, and conversions also matter.
