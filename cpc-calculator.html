Frequently asked questions

Questions about CPC

What is CPC?

CPC means cost per click. It shows the average amount paid for each click.

How do I calculate CPC?

Divide total ad spend by the number of clicks.

Does a low CPC guarantee profit?

No. Profit also depends on conversion rate, customer value, margins, and campaign quality.
