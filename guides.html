How to use the calculators together

The metrics form a chain, and each step changes the next one:

  1. Budget → impressions: budget ÷ CPM × 1,000.
  2. Impressions → clicks: impressions × CTR.
  3. Clicks → cost per click: spend ÷ clicks.
  4. Clicks → conversions: clicks × conversion rate.
  5. Conversions → revenue: orders × average order value.
  6. Revenue → profitability: compare ROAS with break-even ROAS (1 ÷ gross margin), then check net ROI.

A weak link anywhere in that chain shows up as an unprofitable campaign. Most often it is step 4 — the conversion rate on the landing page — not the CPM you negotiated.

How these tools are built

  • Standard, transparent formulas only. Every result shows the arithmetic behind it.
  • Calculations run in your browser; the numbers you type are never uploaded.
  • Benchmark ranges are labelled as reported ranges with their sources, never presented as our own measurements.
  • Estimates are planning tools, not financial advice or a performance guarantee.
