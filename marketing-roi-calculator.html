Frequently asked questions

Questions about Marketing ROI

What is marketing ROI?

Marketing ROI compares the net return from marketing with the marketing cost.

How do I calculate marketing ROI?

Subtract marketing cost from attributed revenue, divide by marketing cost, and multiply by 100.

Is marketing ROI the same as ROAS?

No. ROAS compares revenue with ad spend; ROI subtracts cost before calculating the return percentage.
