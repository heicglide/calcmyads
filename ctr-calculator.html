CTR Calculator formula and meaning

CTR means click-through rate: the percentage of impressions that resulted in a click. Use the calculator above for quick planning, then verify final performance inside your advertising or publishing platform.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about CTR

What is CTR?

CTR means click-through rate: the percentage of impressions that resulted in a click.

How do I calculate CTR?

Divide clicks by impressions and multiply by 100.

What is a good CTR?

It depends on channel, format, placement, audience, and campaign objective. Compare like-for-like campaigns.
