About CalcMyAds

Learn why CalcMyAds builds fast, private advertising calculators.

Our purpose

CalcMyAds turns common advertising formulas into clear, practical tools for marketers, publishers, creators, students, and business owners.

How calculations work

Calculations run locally in your browser. We design every tool to show the formula, the result, and the limitation of the estimate.

Editorial principles

  • Use standard, transparent formulas.
  • Avoid guaranteed performance claims.
  • Separate estimates from platform-reported results.
  • Keep tools accessible and easy to verify.
