Frequently asked questions

Questions about ROAS

What is ROAS?

ROAS measures revenue generated for each unit of advertising spend.

How do I calculate ROAS?

Divide attributed revenue by ad spend. A result of 4 means a 4:1 ROAS, or 400%.

Is ROAS the same as profit?

No. ROAS measures revenue efficiency and does not automatically include product costs, fees, payroll, or overhead.
