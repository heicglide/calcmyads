What is different about advertising a Squarespace store

  • Squarespace does not sell ads. You run campaigns on Google Ads, Meta, TikTok or Pinterest and send the traffic to your Squarespace domain. The ad platform reports the clicks; Squarespace reports the orders.
  • Connect analytics before you spend. Add GA4 (and a Meta pixel if you run Meta ads) through Settings → Advanced → Code Injection, then confirm that a test order appears in both tools. Without this you are optimising blind.
  • Tag every campaign. Use UTM parameters on all ad URLs so Squarespace and GA4 can attribute orders to the right campaign.
  • Check the mobile checkout. Most paid social traffic in the US arrives on mobile. Test the full checkout on a real phone: template blocks, pop-ups and multi-step forms are common causes of a conversion rate stuck below 1.5%.
  • Site speed matters more than template choice. Large hero videos, too many image blocks and third-party scripts all slow the first load, which raises CPC on search and lowers conversion rate on social.

Two rules keep small budgets honest: keep each campaign funded well enough to reach about 50 conversions per week, and cut anything that has spent more than 2–3x your target cost per acquisition with no orders.

Three ways to need less ad spend

  1. Raise average order value. Bundles, a free-shipping threshold and a post-purchase upsell raise AOV, which lowers the clicks needed for the same revenue.
  2. Raise conversion rate. Faster pages, clearer product photography, visible shipping and returns terms, and fewer checkout steps. Going from 2% to 3% cuts required ad spend by a third.
  3. Lower CPC. Tighter keyword and audience targeting, better ad relevance and excluding poor placements. See the CPC calculator.

Then check profitability properly with break-even ROAS in the ROAS calculator — a budget can hit its revenue goal and still lose money.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I spend on ads for a Squarespace store?

Start with what you can spend for 60–90 days without needing a return, usually $500–$1,500 per month. After that, set the budget from a revenue goal using this calculator and keep total paid acquisition near 10–25% of the revenue it produces.

Can I run ads inside Squarespace?

No. Squarespace has email campaigns and SEO features but no ad platform. You buy ads on Google, Meta, TikTok or Pinterest and send traffic to your Squarespace domain.

How do I track ad performance in Squarespace?

Add GA4 and a Meta pixel through Settings → Advanced → Code Injection, tag every ad URL with UTM parameters, and reconcile orders in Squarespace Analytics with the conversions reported by the ad platform. Expect the ad platform to claim more conversions than a last-click or blended view.

What conversion rate should I expect?

US ecommerce sites commonly convert between 1.5% and 3% of sessions, with strong stores above 3%. Use your own last 30 days rather than a target number, and fix the checkout before you scale spend.

Is Google Ads or Meta better for a Squarespace store?

Google captures existing demand (people already searching for the product) and usually converts at a higher rate. Meta creates demand and reaches cheaper impressions. Most stores need both, with retargeting on the platform that produced the first visit.

Why is my required ad spend higher than expected?

Usually a low conversion rate or a low average order value. Both multiply the number of clicks needed. Improving either one lowers the required budget more than bidding harder for cheaper clicks.
