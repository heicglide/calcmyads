eCPM Calculator formula and meaning

eCPM means effective cost per mille. Publishers use it to compare earnings per 1,000 impressions. Use the calculator above for quick planning, then verify final performance inside your advertising or publishing platform.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about eCPM

What is eCPM?

eCPM means effective cost per mille. Publishers use it to compare earnings per 1,000 impressions.

How do I calculate eCPM?

Divide estimated earnings by impressions, then multiply by 1,000.

Is eCPM guaranteed revenue?

No. It is a reporting metric based on the revenue and impressions entered.
