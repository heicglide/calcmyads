Frequently asked questions

Questions about Ad Cost

How is campaign cost calculated from CPM?

Multiply CPM by impressions, then divide by 1,000.

Is this an exact invoice estimate?

No. Platform fees, taxes, auction changes, and adjustments may affect final cost.

Can I use any currency?

Yes. The formula is currency-neutral as long as CPM and cost use the same currency.
